Built to make public opportunities accessible.

Daryaft is driven by a simple goal: bridging the information gap between citizens and the welfare schemes, scholarships and grants meant for them.

Hassan Nadeem

Founder

Currently pursuing a degree in Data Science at NUST, Hassan founded Daryaft to solve a problem he saw firsthand: valuable government and institutional schemes often fail to reach the people who need them most simply because the information is scattered and hard to navigate.

Combining strong data skills with a focus on clear, accessible design, he is building Daryaft to turn complex eligibility criteria into straightforward, actionable answers.

We’re growing.

Daryaft is actively evolving. If you’re passionate about civic tech, data or public policy and want to contribute, reach out to us.

About Daryaft

Making opportunities easier to find.

Opportunities exist everywhere, but finding them isn’t always easy.

01The problem

Across Pakistan, government initiatives, scholarships, financial assistance programmes and public welfare schemes are designed to support individuals and communities. Yet, information about these opportunities is often scattered across different websites, difficult to navigate, or challenging to understand.

Many people miss opportunities not because they aren’t eligible, but because they never find the right information at the right time.

Daryaft aims to change that.

We are building a centralised platform that makes public opportunities easier to discover, understand and access. By bringing relevant information together in one place, Daryaft helps individuals explore available schemes, understand eligibility requirements and find the official resources they need to take the next step.

02Our mission

To make opportunities more accessible by simplifying how people discover and understand public schemes and initiatives.

We believe that access to information should not be a barrier to accessing opportunity. Whether someone is looking for educational assistance, financial support, entrepreneurship programmes or other public initiatives, finding relevant information should be straightforward.

03What we do

Daryaft brings information about public schemes and opportunities into one accessible platform.

Our goal is to make discovering relevant opportunities easier through personalised scheme recommendations.

04Our approach to information

Trust is central to what we are building.

Information about public schemes can change. Eligibility criteria, application deadlines and programme availability may be updated by the authorities responsible for them.

Daryaft aims to make this information easier to understand while maintaining a clear distinction between information presented on our platform and decisions made by official authorities.

We prioritise official government sources and programme announcements when compiling scheme information. We also aim to keep listings updated and direct users to the relevant official application channels.

Where information is unavailable or uncertain, we believe it should be communicated transparently rather than presented as fact.

05What we believe

We believe that opportunity should be accessible to everyone, regardless of where they live or how familiar they are with navigating government systems.

We believe that information should be understandable, transparent and easy to access.

And most importantly, we believe that discovering an opportunity should be the beginning of something, not another obstacle.

06Looking ahead

Daryaft is a growing initiative, and this is only the beginning.

Our vision is to build a platform that makes discovering opportunities across Pakistan simpler, more transparent and more accessible.

Because the right opportunity can change someone’s future. Finding it shouldn’t be the hardest part.
